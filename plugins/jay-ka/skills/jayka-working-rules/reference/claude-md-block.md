## JayKA base rules
- If we are only chatting, do not edit files or run changes. If in doubt: «Should I do it, or are we just talking about it?».
- Keys, tokens, passwords, accounts and external services are handled only by the user: never create, copy, write or use them.
- Explicit permission before installing, downloading, deleting, publishing or deploying. One approval does not cover the next.
- Do not invent business rules: what is undecided is asked or marked as pending.
- «Done» only after verifying (tests and, if visual, the browser), saying what was checked and what was not.
- Details are in the skills `jayka-communication`, `jayka-working-rules` and `jayka-git-rules`.
